## 3A. Planning Artefacts — Outside the Specification Chain

These are **planning artefacts, not specification documents**. They decompose the approved
chain into trackable implementation work and report what that decomposition found. They
**create no requirement, take no business decision, and close no gap**, and they issue no
identifier into the forward traceability chain.

They are registered here so the repository holds a complete inventory. They are **not** part
of the CMP-DOC-01 … CMP-DOC-20 chain and are **not** governed by the baselining order of
CMP-DOC-20 `TRDR-13`. Their lifecycle status is tracked in `Documentation_Status.md` **§5A**,
parallel to §5 and separate from it. Section 3A is lettered rather than numbered so that
existing cross-references to §4, §5 and §6 remain valid.

| ID | Artefact | Short Name | Version | Status | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CMP-PLAN-IMPL-TRACKER | Implementation Work Register | TRACKER | **0.1** | **Draft** | `Document/20_Traceability_Release/CMP-Implementation-Tracker.xlsx` + `.docx` |
| CMP-PLAN-IMPL-ANALYSIS | Implementation Analysis | ANALYSIS | **0.1** | **Draft** | `Document/20_Traceability_Release/CMP-Implementation-Analysis.md` + `.docx` |

> **The workbook is the working artefact.** `CMP-Implementation-Tracker.xlsx` holds the live
> register, the formula-driven dashboard and the tracking sheets the Project Owner maintains.
> `CMP-Implementation-Tracker.docx` is a printable rendering **generated from the workbook**
> for review and sign-off; it is never edited. The dual-format rule of README §8.2 is
> therefore satisfied in substance, with the Excel artefact standing in place of Markdown.

## 6. Index Maintenance Rules

1. This index is updated as part of every document creation or revision.
2. The Version and Status columns must always agree with `Documentation_Status.md`.
3. A document is added to Section 3 with status `Not Started` until its first draft
   exists; from that point Version and Status track the real artifact.
4. Superseded documents remain listed with status `Superseded` and are never deleted
   from the register.
5. Planning artefacts are registered in §3A, never in §3. A planning artefact is not a
   specification document: it may be revised without a change to the specification chain,
   and a change to the chain does not require it to be reissued — though it will be stale
   until it is.
6. An identifier prefix used by any artefact, planning or formal, is registered in §5 so
   that no future document reuses it.
